## Overview

Version 2.0.0 develops *The Butterfly Revolution of Artificial Intelligence* around two central conceptual questions:

1. What does **Breaking the Cocoon** mean as a metaphor for renewed cognitive emergence?
2. Can **new valuable ideas** be understood as a consequential form of humanity's intellectual capital?

The project does not treat the butterfly metaphor as a biological model of AI. It also explicitly distinguishes **Butterfly Revolution** from the scientific **Butterfly Effect**.

## Epistemic Principle

**Novelty ≠ Validity ≠ Usefulness ≠ Ethical Value**

The repository distinguishes established knowledge, documented information, interpretation, inference, hypotheses, proposed frameworks, research questions, speculative scenarios, and philosophical/literary expression.

## Research Status

Conceptual, interdisciplinary, and hypothesis-generating.

No central empirical claim of the Butterfly Revolution framework is presented as scientifically validated by this repository.
