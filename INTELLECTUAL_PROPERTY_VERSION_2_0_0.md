# 3. VERSION 2.0.0 CONCEPTUAL CORE

Version 2.0.0 develops two principal conceptual axes:

### A. Breaking the Cocoon

The **cocoon** is used as a metaphor for inherited epistemic, technological, cultural, and cognitive boundaries.

**Breaking the Cocoon** represents emergence from a previously bounded configuration into a new configuration of possibility.

The associated image of the butterfly is a metaphor for:

- transformation;
- renewed emergence;
- discontinuity;
- expanded degrees of freedom;
- exploration of a changed environment;
- the possibility of a second beginning.

This metaphor is an original conceptual framing within this research project. It should not be confused with the established scientific concept of the butterfly effect in chaos theory.

### B. Ideas as Intellectual Capital

Version 2.0.0 advances the proposition that:

> **New valuable ideas may constitute one of humanity's most consequential forms of intellectual capital.**

This is a conceptual proposition, not a claim that every new idea has economic, scientific, social, or ethical value.

The repository distinguishes:

**Novelty ≠ Validity ≠ Usefulness ≠ Ethical Value**

The value of an idea must therefore remain open to examination, evidence, criticism, validation, and consequences.

# 7. THE BUTTERFLY EFFECT DISTINCTION

The established scientific term **Butterfly Effect** originates in chaos theory and concerns sensitivity to initial conditions.

The present project uses **Butterfly Revolution** as a conceptual expression for transformation and emergence.

Accordingly:

> **Butterfly Revolution ≠ Butterfly Effect**

Any future research connecting these concepts must clearly distinguish established mathematical theory from the project's metaphorical and conceptual use.
